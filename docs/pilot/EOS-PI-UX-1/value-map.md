# Value map

USER: Project Manager / Project Engineer / Engineering Manager

| Page | Question | Value vs a project register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | What is the health, what changed, what needs attention? | Exception-first health, attention queue, change deltas, evidence-linked brief |
| Schedule | What is late or at risk in the published schedule? | Milestone movement and trend over connected schedule evidence; empty if none |
| Cost | What could cost money? | Published exposure and forecast signals; no invented amounts |
| Risk & Change | What is at risk and what is changing? | Combined risk, change, findings, TQ, decision, and action signals |
| Engineering | What engineering work is blocking or overdue? | TQs, document intelligence, meetings, findings, and actions together |
| Decisions | What requires a decision? | Overdue, recent, and dependent decisions from canonical records |
| Reports | What is the executive position? | Interpreted counts and advisory snapshot, not a second truth |
| Ask Project Intelligence | Why, and what evidence supports this? | Grounded answer, limitations, recommended human action |

Documents, Meetings, and Findings remain drill-down. They are not primary destinations for normal PM users.
