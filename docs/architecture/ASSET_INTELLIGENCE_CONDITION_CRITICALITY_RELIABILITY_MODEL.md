# Asset Intelligence — Condition, Criticality, Reliability

## Condition
Answers: *What physical/functional state is the asset currently in?*  
Fields reserved: rating, index, class, confidence, trend, source, observed/approved timestamps, method, evidence refs.  
II condition outputs feed AI via frozen II public contracts; AI must not rewrite original II evidence.

## Criticality
Answers: *How important is this asset?*  
Separate from condition. A healthy asset can be highly critical.

## Reliability
Reserved metrics: availability, failure rate, MTBF, MTTR, PoF, confidence, data window, method, provenance.  
Abstain when evidence is insufficient.

## Risk
AI may emit asset risk *signals/candidates*. Canonical Engineering Risk Register remains Core-owned and human-governed.
