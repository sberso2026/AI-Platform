# EOS-D4-R8 Discipline Interfaces and Handoffs Closeout

## Purpose

R8 governs the exact information Mechanical hands to or receives from other disciplines. A handoff preserves source truth and authority boundaries. It does not fabricate a missing counterpart, silently repoint history, validate the other discipline, or perform the multidisciplinary reasoning reserved for D9.

R1 through R7 remain the earlier Mechanical runtimes at counts one through seven. R8 is the eighth Mechanical runtime and the one discipline-interface runtime. Individual discipline handoffs are not separate runtimes. D4-0 architecture runtime flags stay at their historical zero. D1 structural pack complete remains no. D1N stays closed. Civil and Geotechnical remain complete and are not reopened.

## Canonical interface governance

Handoff status uses the canonical Civil handoff vocabulary. Silent repointing stays prohibited by the canonical structural interface rule. An acknowledged handoff is not engineering approval. AI cannot approve an interface. Fingerprints use the canonical fingerprint. Currentness uses the canonical currentness states. No parallel interface, handoff, currentness, or fingerprint engine is created.

## Exact D4 R1-R7 binding

Every handoff names an R1 context, an R1 object and that object's exact revision fingerprint, an R2 subject and revision fingerprint, and the exact R3, R4, R5, and R6 record fingerprints. A cited R7 change must name that change and its exact fingerprint. Floating references such as latest or current are rejected as revisions. Historical handoffs stay bound to the revision they cited.

## D4-D1, D4-D2, and D4-D3 handoffs

Mechanical may record a governed handoff toward Structural, Civil, or Geotechnical using the architecture subject lists. Where a counterpart record already exists, the handoff cites that record's exact revision and fingerprint. A Mechanical load context is not Structural design. A handoff does not transfer Structural, Civil, or Geotechnical authority and does not validate that counterpart. D1, D2, and D3 remain the authorities for their own engineering.

## D5-D8 future contracts

Piping, Process, Electrical, and Instrumentation and Control contracts are defined from the D4-0 subject lists. Those disciplines remain unstarted. A nozzle context is not piping design. Mechanical does not create an authoritative process condition. An electrical or control interface context is not that discipline's design. Authority is not transferred. No counterpart record is created for a discipline that has no runtime.

## Missing counterparts and currentness

An explicit counterpart reference is required when a counterpart is cited. Matching tags, a shared vendor, a shared package, system membership, proximity, document co-occurrence, similar values, and semantic similarity do not create a counterpart. A missing counterpart is not fabricated and is not treated as a complete interface. Currentness, applicability, limitations, source authority, counterpart authority, provenance, and history are stored on the handoff. A stale handoff is not exposed as current. An incomplete handoff is not exposed as complete.

## Change, mutation, and history

A handoff may cite an exact R7 change. When that change requires reassessment, the handoff records reassessment required and does not recalculate, redesign, or validate the engineering. R3 design and performance values, R5 requirements, and R6 maintainability statements stay unchanged. An explicit successor marks the predecessor superseded and leaves the original source revision and fingerprint in history. Supersession does not delete the record and does not silently point an old handoff at a new revision.

## Authority and later-phase boundaries

AI and admin are not Mechanical engineering authority. AI cannot fabricate a counterpart or promote a handoff to engineering validated. An administrative action cannot do that either. A created handoff is not reviewed, validated, or approved. Review, validation, conformance, approval, and certification remain at count zero. The dedicated currentness and digital-thread runtime remains at count zero. The D9 source contract and cross-discipline reasoning, conflict detection, conflict resolution, and recommendation remain at count zero. Equipment sizing, the other Mechanical calculations, spatial clash detection, access and removal solvers, layout optimization, and automatic redesign remain at count zero. Mechanical handoffs do not become Structural, Civil, Geotechnical, Piping, Process, Electrical, or Instrumentation and Control engineering. D5 through D9 remain unstarted. No schema change. No new dependency. Review, the currentness thread, and the D9 source contract remain not ready.

## Integrity

Duplicate handoffs, replay, a wrong or stale source revision, a wrong counterpart revision, a fabricated or inferred counterpart, an invalid discipline pair, authority spoofing, provenance spoofing, cross-project and cross-tenant reference, fingerprint mismatch, and historical auto-repointing are rejected. Replay of the same handoff and fingerprint returns the existing record.
