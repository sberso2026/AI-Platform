# EOS D1 structural pack completion criteria and roadmap sequencing

## Decision

This is a founder-authorized governance record. It defines when the D1 Structural pack may later be called complete, and it authorizes the next discipline architecture phase. It does not remediate D1 debt, reopen D1M or D1N, complete the Structural pack, or start Civil, Geotechnical, Mechanical, Piping, Process, Electrical, Instrumentation and Control, or cross-discipline runtime.

Four states stay distinct. Track complete means one D1 track has a bounded closeout. Pack implementation complete means the required D1 capability tracks are implemented. Pilot ready means a pilot claim is authorized. General availability ready means the release gate for general availability is satisfied. Those states are not the same. Pack completion is not engineering certification, not project certification eligibility, and not general availability.

## Current D1 state

D1N remains complete with limitations and is not reopened. D1M remains complete with limitations and is not reopened. D1L remains complete with limitations. D1K internal discipline integration and cross-discipline intelligence remain not ready. The Structural pack remains incomplete. D2 through D8 runtime count remains zero. D9 runtime count remains zero. The historical D1N closeout statement that no successor was canonical at that closeout stays unchanged.

## Pack completion criteria

The D1 Structural pack becomes complete only when all of the following are true.

1. The required D1 capability tracks are implemented and have bounded closeouts.
2. The qualified Structural engineering review required by C10 and by D1F is complete.
3. A separately authorized D1G validation route is complete. The current route phase remains not defined, and the solver remains not certified.
4. D1H software engineer validation and method conformance are complete.
5. D1I software engineer validation and conformance are complete.
6. D1J engineer validation and conformance are complete.
7. D1K Structural-internal integration criteria are satisfied.
8. The D1L certification-governance capability is implemented.
9. No unresolved blocker remains classified as blocking D1 pack completion.
10. The mandatory security and release regression gate passes for that completion phase.

Pack completion does not require D1K cross-discipline intelligence, completion of D2 through D8, completion of D9, a project-specific certification, or resolution of the GA security debt.

## Debt sequencing

C10 qualified human review and D1F engineer review block D1 pack completion. They do not block the start of D2 Civil. The D1G validation route is required for D1 pack completion and is not required before D2 starts. D1H, D1I, and D1J validation block D1 pack completion and do not block D2. No repository evidence shows those items are a direct prerequisite of D2 Civil architecture.

D1 debt may be remediated in parallel with D2 through D8. Each remediation phase needs its own scope, authority, tests, security and release gate, closeout, and roadmap update. Remediation must not silently rewrite a closed D1 track.

## D1K and D9

D1K owns Structural interface evidence and governance. D1K does not own global cross-discipline reasoning. Structural-internal D1K integration is a D1 pack concern. Cross-discipline intelligence is a D9 concern. That intelligence is not required to start D2. It is required before D9 can be treated as ready. D9 owns cross-discipline intelligence. D9 stays not ready, with runtime count zero, until the required D1 through D8 source contracts exist and the cross-discipline authority, interface, currentness, conflict, and human-authority models are defined.

## Discipline order

The pack order remains D1 Structural, D2 Civil, D3 Geotechnical, D4 Mechanical, D5 Piping, D6 Process, D7 Electrical, D8 Instrumentation and Control, and D9 cross-discipline intelligence. D2 through D8 may proceed through their own architecture and implementation tracks before the D1 pack is complete, while unresolved D1 limitations stay explicit. Shared platform blockers and security regressions still block the phases they affect. Actual cross-discipline integration stays with D9 unless a later decision authorizes a narrower interface. This decision does not start D3 through D8.

GA security debt still blocks general availability. It does not block D2 start, D3 through D8 development, or D9 development. This decision does not weaken a security regression gate and does not declare general availability.

## Next phase

The next canonical discipline is D2 Civil. The next phase is EOS-D2-0-CIVIL-ARCHITECTURE-CANONICALIZATION. Its type is civil architecture canonicalization. It will define Civil scope, capability boundaries, object ownership, relationships to D1, interfaces required for later D9, jurisdiction and standards extensibility, lifecycle and currentness, authority boundaries, human oversight, evidence and provenance, and an implementation decomposition. It adds no substantive Civil runtime until that architecture is approved. This governance phase does not execute it.

D1 structural pack complete remains no. D1 structural pack completion criteria are now defined.
