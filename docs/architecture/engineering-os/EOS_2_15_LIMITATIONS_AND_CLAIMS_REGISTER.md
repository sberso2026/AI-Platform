# EOS 2.15 Limitations and Claims Register

## Permitted claim

EOS 2.0 is a validated and bounded-qualified multidisciplinary engineering delivery, traceability, interface, change-impact, lifecycle, configuration and deliverable-governance platform for controlled engineering workflows across Concept, Feasibility, FEED and Detailed Engineering.

The hosted evidence for that sentence is preview `dpl_Am7AhnHwv7Hjdymx4e73AXrfv75C` of runtime commit `af6612bdebe40616dab18525ceab4dc70f03621e`.

## Required qualification language

- The qualification is bounded.
- Unsupported deterministic calculations stay outside the claim.
- Interface acceptance, materiality, freeze, deliverable approval, IFC release, and change closure require a named human authority.
- Imported solver results are provenance records, not EOS calculations.
- AI may explain evidence. AI does not approve, accept, finalize materiality, freeze, release, recalculate, or mutate a baseline.

## Unsupported claims

- EOS performs all engineering calculations.
- EOS replaces HYSYS, CAESAR II, ETAP, or any other external solver.
- EOS automatically certifies designs or guarantees regulatory compliance.
- EOS autonomously approves engineering.
- EOS provides certified SIL calculations or qualified geotechnical numerical analysis.
- EOS provides full enterprise document management or complete construction management.
- EOS holds SOC 2, ISO, or another certification that has not been issued.
- A synthetic fixture or an AI explanation is engineering verification.
- A candidate impact is already a material engineering impact.
- Receiver acceptance is design approval. Freeze or IFC issue is regulatory certification.

## External-tool dependent capabilities

Process simulation, piping stress, structural analysis outside D1, civil design surfaces, geotechnical numerical analysis, electrical system studies, and instrument or control configuration remain dependent on external tools. Current adapters are metadata or import only and are not certified. `EXTERNAL_ENGINEERING_SOLVER_EXECUTION_GLOBALLY_QUALIFIED=NO`.

## Unqualified calculations

Process thermodynamics, hydraulics, relief, and valve sizing. Mechanical vessel, pump, fatigue, nozzle, and rotordynamic calculations. Piping sizing and stress. Civil grading, drainage, pavement, and flood calculations. Geotechnical bearing, settlement, pile, slope, liquefaction, and excavation calculations. Electrical load flow, short circuit, protection, cable sizing, arc flash, earthing, and UPS calculations. I&C SIL, instrument sizing, control tuning, and logic generation.

## Residual limitations after QD-001

- The preview remains behind Vercel deployment protection. Qualification used the authorized automation bypass and then EOS login. Staging was not made public. Production protection was not changed.
- Governed receiver and candidate-impact tables do not grant update to the application role. The hosted decision is an inserted row. The original pending or unassessed row remains.
- `str-dwg` was already `potentially_stale` on the synthetic fixture. The application left revision 1 in place, did not withdraw it, and did not rewrite the FEED baseline.
- P-101 mass and power for this path are interface values. They are not a new mechanical calculation.
- The qualification tenant has staging-only subscription, licence, seat, and installation rows so the existing commerce gate can open the synthetic workspace. Those rows are fixture data. They are not a migration and they were not copied from production.
- The bounded answers come from the hosted qualification API. An external language model was not invoked.

## Synthetic fixture statement

Synthetic fixtures validate workflow behavior and provenance handling. They do not qualify engineering calculation correctness unless independently benchmarked against authoritative reference results.
