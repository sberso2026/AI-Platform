# EOS-D1J-R5 Structural Optimization Certification Closeout

This closeout records the bounded D1J track. A closed track is not a full optimization product. A bounded runtime is not an optimizer. An implemented readiness gate is not certification eligibility. Certification eligibility is not a certified design.

## Phase history

The canonical sequence is architecture, problem and baseline and variable foundation, objective and constraint and feasibility references, candidate lineage and comparison composition, certification-evidence readiness, and this closeout. Architecture remains defined. R1 records the problem, baseline, variables, and variable set. R2 records objectives, constraints, required checks, evidence references, and feasibility without executing checks. R3 records candidate identity, lineage, evidence correlation, comparison eligibility, and non-dominated composition. R4 records human review, human selection, the study manifest, selected-candidate evidence, completeness, and the readiness gate. Each phase reused the previous runtime.

## Final capability and maturity

Implemented bounded records cover problem definition, baseline, variable shape, objectives, constraints, check and analysis and value references, feasibility orchestration, candidate identity and lineage, candidate evidence, comparison eligibility, Pareto composition, human review, human selection, the study manifest, and the readiness assessment. Concrete model-parameter assignment is not available. Automatic candidate generation, optimization search, solver-in-the-loop optimization, design-model mutation, automatic selection, and professional certification are not implemented. Certification eligibility assessment exists and its outcome remains not ready.

The track status is complete bounded with limitations. The maturity is a bounded structural optimization certification runtime. That maturity does not imply an optimizer and does not imply certification eligibility. Release classification remains an internal engineering reference. It is not general availability, not certification ready, not solver certified, not engineer validated, and not conformance validated.

## Readiness and eligibility

Architecture is ready. Fixture tests of the bounded runtime are ready and are not live validation. Pilot runtime, live solver runtime, engineer validation, and conformance validation are not ready. Current certification eligibility is NOT_ELIGIBLE. The blockers are the uncertified D1G solver, absent D1J engineer validation, and absent D1J conformance validation. Parameter-semantic debt is disclosed and is not an additional eligibility gate.

## Claim scope

Evidence claims stay separate. A candidate-evidence claim, a feasibility claim, a multi-objective comparison claim, a Pareto composition claim, a selected-alternative claim, and a certification-eligibility claim are not interchangeable. A comparison-ineligible candidate may keep its feasibility evidence and cannot support a comparative optimization claim. Comparison ineligibility does not erase feasibility.

Dominance is not infeasibility and is not engineering acceptance. A dominated feasible candidate may be human-selected with explicit rationale. A non-dominated candidate is not selected, approved, or eligible by that status alone. Human selection is required for a final study alternative. An AI actor and an optimizer cannot make that selection. Selection is not design approval. Formal approval stays in the existing engineering-review decision workflow.

## Preserved debt

R1 variable kinds name governed shapes. They do not name a concrete model property. Candidates remain reference-only and are not EOS-generated designs. Evidence for a candidate must match that candidate design-state fingerprint. Baseline evidence is not reused as candidate evidence.

No optimization algorithm, candidate generator, solver execution, engineering-check execution, or model mutation is added by this closeout. D1G remains not certified, not live validated, not engineer validated, and not conformance validated. Offline connector conformance is not solver certification. D1J engineer-validation evidence count is zero. D1J conformance-validation evidence count is zero. A study review does not promote software validation. A study selection does not promote conformance validation.

Future enablement still requires concrete parameter semantics before model assignment, an implemented search only under a later authorized phase, a certified D1G solver before solver-backed claims, and independent D1J engineer and conformance validation before certification eligibility can change.

## Security and next roadmap

The known EOS-A16D security debt remains. It blocks general availability and does not block this bounded closeout. No database migration is required.

D1K Cross-Discipline Structural Interfaces remains not started. Its recorded dependencies are D1A and D1F. D1L Structural Global Certification Gate remains not started. D1M and D1N remain unabsorbed boundaries. The structural pack is not complete. D2 is not started. The post-D1I roadmap authorized D1J architecture and did not authorize a successor after this closeout. This closeout does not invent that successor and does not start D1K. The next action is a separate roadmap governance decision.
