## What review and validation govern

Every review request, finding, disposition, completion, and validation record binds to an exact R1 identity, the exact R2 stream or system, and one exact subject revision from R1 through R8. A request stays requested. Completion is a separate record and does not mark the request validated. Findings keep an exact reviewed revision, a canonical disposition, reviewer authority, provenance, and history. A disposition of noted or reassessment-requested does not close the finding as validated, approved, or conformant. Validation requires an exact subject revision, an explicit basis, and a traceable validation authority. Validated is not conformant, approved, certified, or regulatory-approved. Canonical review statuses, finding categories, validation states, the pre-issue authority boundary, human-actor checks, currentness, digital thread, and fingerprint are reused. No parallel review or validation engine is created.

## Boundaries that stay closed

A requested review is not a completed review, and a completed review is not validation. A finding does not invalidate the Process design and does not fail validation. Closing or accepting a finding is not validation, approval, or conformance. Reassessment required is not validation failure. Reassessment completed is not validated or approved. A prior review or validation does not transfer onto a new revision. Stale and superseded validation are not exposed as current, and a reassessment-required record is not exposed as validated. An R8 handoff may be the review subject. Reviewing or recording a validation state for that handoff does not validate Mechanical, Piping, Electrical, or I&C engineering. External Process evidence and external tool results do not determine validation. Process duty is not a validated Mechanical selection. Process safety context is not functional-safety validation, a relief scenario is not a validated relief device, and a safeguard requirement is not a validated SIF. Validation does not approve, certify, or determine conformance. AI and admin are not reviewer authority, validation authority, or Process engineering authority. AI cannot close a review as engineering-validated or set an authoritative validation state. Admin cannot promote a review to validation. Historical fingerprints are not auto-repointed, and silent mutation is rejected. Tenant, workspace, and project scope is enforced. Duplicate, replay, and spoofed authority, revision, provenance, and fingerprint attempts are rejected.
