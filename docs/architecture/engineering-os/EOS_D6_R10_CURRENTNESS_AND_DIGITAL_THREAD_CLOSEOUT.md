## What currentness and the digital thread govern

Every currentness mark, supersession, and lineage edge binds to an exact R1 through R9 revision. Current, stale, superseded, and reassessment-required states are recorded as overlays. The underlying engineering records stay immutable. A current record is not validated, approved, conformant, or certified. A stale record is not invalid or rejected. A superseded record is not invalid, rejected, deleted, or exposed as current. A reassessment-required record is not exposed as validated or fully current. Lineage uses an explicit relationship or a governed relation. Shared identity, matching values, common documents, semantic similarity, timing, system membership, and AI interpretation do not create lineage. Canonical currentness states, relation semantics, digital-thread depth, and fingerprint are reused. No parallel currentness engine, digital thread, or fingerprint algorithm is created.

## Boundaries that stay closed

A new identity revision does not repoint history and does not invalidate the prior revision. A new system, stream, or service context does not rewrite a historical relationship, and a stale context is not exposed as current. A new source, operating, or design condition does not replace an adopted or prior condition. New external evidence does not replace prior evidence, and stale evidence is not exposed as current. New duty or utility evidence does not replace an adopted duty or utility requirement. New relief evidence does not replace a relief scenario. A new hazard-study output does not close a prior finding. New SIL or LOPA evidence does not replace a prior safety requirement. A superseded record stays in history. A stale or superseded handoff is not exposed as current, and the interface counterpart revision is not repointed or mutated. A prior review does not transfer to a new revision, and a stale review is not the current review. A prior validation does not transfer to a new revision. Stale and superseded validation are not exposed as current. A fingerprint mismatch is detected and is not repaired or rebaselined. Currentness does not mutate R1 through R9 records and does not trigger recalculation, redesign, revalidation, or approval. AI and admin are not Process engineering authority. AI cannot invent lineage or repoint history. Admin cannot treat a fingerprint mismatch as valid. Tenant, workspace, and project scope is enforced. Duplicate lineage, duplicate currentness, replay, and spoofed stale, superseded, fingerprint, lineage, authority, scope, provenance, and counterpart revisions are rejected.
