# EOS 3.0 Non-Goals

`EOS_3_NON_GOALS_DEFINED=YES`

EOS 3.0 will **not**:

1. Become a full ERP (finance, procurement, HR, inventory as system of record).
2. Become a full construction management platform (schedule/cost/contract packages as primary product).
3. Replace CAD/BIM authoring tools.
4. Replace all discipline engineering solvers or claim universal solver qualification.
5. Provide autonomous professional engineering practice or PE/regulatory certification engines.
6. Become a generic enterprise DMS (SharePoint/Aconex/Documentum replacement).
7. Grant unrestricted AI agent autonomy (approval, IFC, release, certification, final materiality).
8. Silently auto-migrate EOS 2 GA customers/projects to EOS 3 (`EOS_2_AUTO_MIGRATION_TO_EOS_3=NO`).
9. Clear the EOS 2 external-customer legal hold (reserved for `EOS-2.23-…`).
10. Broaden Structural D1 qualification by implication; D1 remains `PRESERVE_COMPATIBLE` / `QUALIFIED_BOUNDED` unless requalified.
11. Treat embeddings/vector stores as authoritative engineering state.
12. Invent a generic unconstrained no-code workflow engine without governed engineering purpose.
