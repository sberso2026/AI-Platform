# Risk-Prioritized My Work Engine

Determines what requires an engineer’s attention, what to look at first, why, consequence if ignored, and the next engineering action.

Priority represents **engineering consequence and obligation**, not newest-first, due-date-only, or opaque AI ranking.

Core prioritization is deterministic and does not require a live LLM.
Does not replace the enterprise Risk register or schedule critical path.
Does not grant authority.
