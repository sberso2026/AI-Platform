## Upstream binding

## Context and recommendations

Foundation context stores supplied founding range, applicability, limitations, and an optional recommendation. It is not a structural foundation design. Excavation context stores a supplied depth range and constraints without designing support. Slope and geohazard context stores a supplied observation without a stability analysis. Ground-improvement context stores a supplied method category without selecting or designing a system. A recommendation is either absent, an explicit candidate, or a governed adoption. An adopted recommendation is not validated, approved, or certified. An AI-generated statement may remain only a candidate. AI and administrator actors are not Geotechnical engineering authority. A record without governed evidence is rejected.
