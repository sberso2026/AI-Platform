# EOS-EU-D0 Discipline Capability Framework Review

Assurance review of the EOS-D0 common discipline framework against global deployment and EU high-water-mark architecture. Not EU market certification. Not country legal sign-off. EOS remains one Engineering OS plus installable discipline packs.

## Decision

The D0 framework is the canonical basis for D1–D9 after bounded additive contract closures (interface object/provenance fields, inspection finding classes, AI pack declaration fields, classifiable AI data, person-twin lock, output transparency labels, and this risk register).

D1 must still start with a **bounded Structural subphase plan**. It must not attempt all Structural capability in one phase.

## Global-first

No mandatory D0 field assumes EU-only, Australia-only, or US-only jurisdiction, one code family, one privacy regime, or one data region. Packs inherit jurisdiction, standards, AI, privacy, security, and provenance profiles. National annexes remain jurisdiction-selected.

## Residual constraints for D1

- SPACE GASS live execution uncertified; no silent fallback
- No AS/EN/AISC/ACI capacity engines
- Synthetic UDL demand only
- Member/connection/frame/inspection/twin models not implemented
- Calculation standard edition/annex not yet bound
- Optimization not certified as a discipline engine
- Profile A pilot limitations unchanged

## Roadmap

Recommended pack order remains D1 Structural → D2 Civil → D3 Geotechnical → D4 Mechanical → D5 Piping → D6 Process → D7 Electrical → D8 I&C → D9 Cross-Discipline Intelligence.

Live interface data often flows Process → Mechanical → Piping → Structural → Geotechnical. That is a future intelligence dependency, not a reason to silently reorder D1–D8. Structural remains the reference pack and goes first.
