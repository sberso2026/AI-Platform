## Canonical reuse

Currentness reuses the architecture states CURRENT, STALE, REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED, and SUPERSEDED. No parallel currentness engine was created. Lineage relationship codes reuse the governed digital-thread relation catalogue, and Civil identity edges reuse PARENT and OBJECT_REFERENCE. Explicit change classes reuse the architecture dependency list. Propagation depth reuses the digital-thread default depth. Fingerprints reuse fingerprintCanonical. No second fingerprint algorithm and no parallel Civil digital thread were created.

## Exact revision semantics

Every lineage endpoint, supersession endpoint, and currentness mark pins a subject kind, subject id, subject revision, and subject fingerprint. Object revisions are the object fingerprint. Requirement revisions are the stored source revision together with the requirement fingerprint. Handoff revisions are the stored source revision together with the handoff fingerprint. Review and validation revisions remain the subject revision those records already cite. Floating labels, including latest, are rejected. A missing surface, grading, road, drainage, or utility context is not invented.

## Lineage

A lineage edge records source and target identity and revision, a governed relationship type, an optional explicit Civil dependency class, provenance, authority, timestamp, fingerprint, and history. Inferred, proximity, similar-name, and chronology edges are rejected. An AI actor may record only a non-authoritative candidate. An AI actor cannot create an authoritative dependency.

## Supersession

A supersession cites the predecessor revision and the successor revision. The predecessor is not deleted and is not marked engineering-invalid. Existing lineage edges keep the revision they originally cited. Supersession does not rewrite those edges and does not replace every reference with the successor.

## Stale and reassessment state

A currentness mark may be CURRENT, STALE, REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED, or SUPERSEDED. Stale is not engineering-invalid and is not an engineering rejection. Reassessment required is not engineering-invalid. The mark does not change engineering content.

## Explicit propagation

Propagation walks only authoritative lineage edges whose relationship is an explicit governed code, and only to the recorded depth. A surface revision can mark an explicitly dependent grading object, and that grading object can mark an explicitly dependent road, drainage, utility, handoff, review, finding, or validation record. An object with no explicit edge is not marked. A candidate edge is not propagated. Propagation writes currentness marks only. It does not change object fingerprints, requirement text, handoff source revisions, or review and validation subject revisions.

## R7, R8, and R9 integration

A requirement, assumption, or decision can be an explicit upstream endpoint. Marking one of those records can mark the explicitly linked Civil object as reassessment required. It does not redesign that object. A handoff can become stale through an explicit edge and keeps its original source revision. A review request, finding, and validation record can be marked stale through an explicit edge and remain bound to the original subject revision. Prior review and prior validation are not copied onto a successor revision. Evidence lineage can cite more than one evidence object. A later evidence edge does not remove the earlier evidence edge and does not make the engineering validated.

## Authority, provenance, fingerprint, history, and scope

AI is not Civil engineering authority and cannot silently repoint history. An administrator is not Civil engineering authority. Provenance is required and stored. History is append-only. Tenant, workspace, and project must match the actor. Replay of the same id and fingerprint returns the existing record. A conflicting fingerprint or a cross-scope actor is rejected.
