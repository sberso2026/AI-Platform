## Systems of record

- EOS: engineering digital thread + configuration provenance
- External EAM/CMMS: maintenance execution/work orders/spares
- DMS: controlled documents
- Historian: process/time-series (references only in EOS)

EOS is not a full construction ERP, EAM, CMMS, or historian replacement.
