## 5. Human authority model

AI is not cross-discipline engineering authority and is not D9 engineering authority. Admin is not cross-discipline engineering authority and is not D9 engineering authority. An AI inference is not an engineering decision. A recommendation is not an engineering decision. An escalation is not approval. ## 6. Source fact versus inference

A source fact stays owned by its source discipline and keeps source discipline, exact revision, provenance, and limitations. A D9 inference is a separately governed derived artefact. It requires source references, exact source revisions, provenance, applicability, and limitations. A source fact is not a D9 inference. A D9 inference is not an engineering fact and is not engineering validation. No inference runtime is implemented in D9-0.

## 7. Conflict candidate versus confirmed conflict

The governed record classes are source-fact reference, cross-discipline observation, inference, conflict candidate, confirmed conflict, escalation, recommendation, reassessment requirement, and resolution evidence. An observation is not a confirmed conflict. A conflict candidate is not a confirmed engineering conflict. A future candidate must retain both source disciplines, both exact revisions, applicable rules or basis, provenance, the detected inconsistency, confidence or uncertainty where canonical, limitations, and currentness. No conflict candidate is generated in D9-0.

Conflict classes are architecture labels only: requirement inconsistency, interface mismatch, assumption incompatibility, design-basis mismatch, currentness mismatch, validation-state mismatch, source revision mismatch, operating or design condition inconsistency, and physical or interface compatibility. D9-0 does not detect them.

## 8. Conflict detection versus resolution

A detected conflict is not a resolved conflict. A confirmed conflict is not a resolved conflict. Confirmation must be governed and traceable by an authorized human. Resolution remains a separate governed process. Automatic conflict resolution count is 0.

## 9. Escalation semantics

A future escalation identifies the candidate or conflict, source disciplines, exact revisions, reason, responsible discipline or human authority, required action, status, source basis, provenance, applicability, and limitations. Severity or priority is included only where the canonical record already has it. An escalation is not an engineering decision, not approval, and not conflict resolution.

## 10. Recommendation versus decision

A future recommendation requires source traceability and limitations. A recommendation is not an engineering decision, not approval, and not an automatic design change. D9 reuses canonical decision governance. No parallel D9 engineering-decision engine is created. Any future authoritative decision stays explicit and human-authorized. Recommendation runtime count and engineering-decision runtime count are 0.

## 11. Currentness propagation

Future derived artefacts keep the exact source revision set used. If an explicitly dependent source changes, the architecture may record stale, superseded, or reassessment-required. A source change is not an invalid result, a recalculated result, or a revalidated result. A source change does not automatically recompute reasoning, regenerate a recommendation, or resolve a conflict. Recomputation stays governed. D9 reuses the canonical currentness states. No parallel currentness engine is created.

## 12. Exact source revision set

Every future inference, conflict record, and recommendation must be reproducible from an exact source revision set. Floating source revisions are prohibited. Historical references are not automatically repointed. History preservation is required.

## 13. Digital thread, lineage, and fingerprint

D9 reuses the canonical digital-thread module and the canonical fingerprint algorithm. No parallel digital thread and no second fingerprint algorithm are created. Explicit lineage is required. Inferred lineage is prohibited.

## 14. Provenance, applicability, and limitations

Provenance is required and must be preserved back to the discipline source contracts. Source applicability must be preserved and must not be propagated outside that applicability. Source limitations must be preserved and must not be silently dropped. This includes incomplete or bounded tracks such as D1.

## 15. Human oversight
