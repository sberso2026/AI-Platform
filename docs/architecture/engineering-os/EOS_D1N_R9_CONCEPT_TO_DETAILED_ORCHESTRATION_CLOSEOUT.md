# EOS-D1N-R9 concept-to-detailed orchestration closeout

## Purpose

D1N records governed Structural progression from concept through detailed design. It identifies the orchestration context, the stated progression stage, the prerequisite evidence and gaps, a transition request, a human decision, an alternative branch, whether those records are still current, and the exact D1M, D1K, and D1L records it may cite. It does not validate, approve, calculate, certify, interpret CAD or BIM, or own those upstream tracks.

## R1 through R8

R1 records one orchestration context with scope, provenance, and fingerprint. R2 records an explicit progression stage and does not advance it. R3 records prerequisite status and evidence gaps and does not decide stage readiness. R4 records a transition request and does not approve it or change the stage. R5 records an external human gate decision and does not execute the transition. R6 records an alternative branch and a citation of an existing selection decision, and does not rank or choose the best design. R7 records currentness and reassessment against explicit dependencies and does not refresh historical references. R8 cites exact D1M, D1K, and D1L records under that currentness and does not absorb them. Closeout adds no ninth runtime. The runtime count remains 8.

## Progression model

The stages remain CONCEPT, PREFEASIBILITY, FEASIBILITY, FEED, and DETAILED_DESIGN. A recorded stage is not engineering validation, engineering approval, conformance, certification, or release.

## Digital thread

The thread is D1A identity, then the R1 context, the R2 progression, the R3 assessment, the R4 request, the R5 human decision, the R6 branch, the R7 currentness record, and the R8 handoff citations. Fingerprints and history stay on the canonical mechanisms. No second thread is created.

## Authority

The recorder of D1N context, progression, assessment, request, branch, currentness, and handoff is orchestration-context authority. The human gate remains lifecycle-gate decision authority with assurance level aal2. D1M, D1K, and D1L authorities stay on the cited records. AI and admin are not engineering authority.

## Currentness

R7 remains the currentness model. Stale and reassessment-required states do not rewrite the prior record, repoint it to a newer dependency, revalidate engineering, reapprove a transition, reselect a branch, or recertify.

## Upstream boundaries

D1M remains complete with limitations and is not reopened. A D1M citation is not CAD or BIM interpretation. D1K internal integration and cross-discipline intelligence stay not ready, and D1K limitations stay visible. D1L remains complete with limitations. A D1L citation does not determine eligibility, issue certification, or revoke certification.

## Boundary matrix

Stage-transition execution, engineering validation, engineering approval, solver execution, conformance, certification, calculation authorship, and CAD or BIM interpretation remain at count zero. D1N absorbs none of D1M, D1K, or D1L. D9 and D2 through D8 remain at zero. No schema and no dependency are added.
