# Asset Intelligence — Shared Domain Boundary

## Shared Engineering Domain (canonical)
Owns `EngineeringAsset` / `engineering_assets`, types, hierarchy refs, Core service/UI, KG linkage.

## Asset Intelligence (future product)
Owns versioned intelligence states: condition, criticality, reliability, risk signals, failure, degradation, lifecycle intelligence, maintenance recommendations.

## Forbidden
- Creating a second asset identity register
- Silently mutating Shared Domain identity fields from AI
- Direct coupling to II private schemas
- Claiming Digital Twin or CMMS ownership
