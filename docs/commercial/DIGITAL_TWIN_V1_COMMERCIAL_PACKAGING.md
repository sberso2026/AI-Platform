# Digital Twin V1.0 — Commercial Packaging

## Capability groups (marketable)

1. **Digital Twin Core** — identity, lookup, relationships
2. **Twin State & History** — state, snapshots, timeline
3. **Telemetry Binding** — binding/projection over asset_intelligence time series
4. **Representation & Navigation** — mapping and navigation (no BIM/CAD authoring)
5. **Digital Thread** — refs-only provenance composition
6. **Simulation Governance** — methods, scenarios, packages, four-layer qualification
7. **Engineering Simulation Integration** — CalculiX linear static external adapter

## Explicit commercial exclusions

Do not market: GIS runtime, SHM, predictive twin, PoF, RUL, optimization, physical actuation, automatic control, native engineering solvers, high-frequency historian, sensor registry, Project Controls, Asset Intelligence product surfaces, BIM/CAD authoring, modal/buckling/thermal/nonlinear/contact/dynamic/fatigue/fracture/CFD solvers.
