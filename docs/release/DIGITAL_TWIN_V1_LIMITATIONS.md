# Digital Twin V1.0 — Limitations

## Advisory intelligence

Simulation results are SIMULATED. Digital Thread is refs-only. Telemetry binding reuses asset_intelligence time series and does not store raw streams.

## Forbidden engines

Physical actuation, automatic control, predictive twin, PoF/RUL, SHM, GIS, optimization, and native engineering solvers remain unavailable. CalculiX is limited to linear elastic static.
